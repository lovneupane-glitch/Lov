A LITTLE ABOUT THIS SITE

Welcome to
My Media Gallery.

My Media Gallery is a personal media-sharing website created to keep videos, audio and photos organised in one easy-to-use place. Visitors can explore and download the public collection, while uploads are reserved for the site administrator.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Can anyone visit?

Yes. The gallery is public, so visitors do not need an account.

Can visitors upload files?

No. Only the authorised administrator can upload files after signing in.

Can I download media?

Yes. Use the Download link on a media card. Please respect the creator's rights.

What can I find here?

Videos, audio and photos, organised by category and newest upload date.
